TubeArchive Pro

This wizard installs the program together with the helper programs it needs:
  - yt-dlp   (reads and downloads the videos)
  - ffmpeg / ffprobe   (joins video and audio, converts to MP3, WAV, AVI ...)

Your settings, favorites, download history and downloaded ads are kept in
  %APPDATA%\TubeArchivePro
so a new version can be installed over the old one without losing them.

Close TubeArchive Pro before installing a new version.
