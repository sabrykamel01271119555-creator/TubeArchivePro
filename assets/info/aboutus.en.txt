# About TubeArchive Pro
> Your personal YouTube archive - download, organise and keep up to date.

~ Version {version}  ·  2026  ·  Developed by Sabry Kamel

## What the application does
TubeArchive Pro is a Windows application that downloads YouTube videos, audio tracks and complete playlists in the quality you choose, and keeps everything organised so you can always find it again.

## Main features
- **Download videos and audio** - MP4, MKV, WEBM, AVI for video; MP3 and WAV for audio, in the qualities that the video really offers.
- **Smart download queue** - several files download at the same time (you choose how many); the rest wait and start by themselves. Pause, resume, cancel or skip any file.
- **Playlists** - a playlist is downloaded video by video, numbered 01, 02, 03 ..., with a progress bar for the current file and for the whole playlist.
- **Playlist Sync** - the application checks your playlists on the schedule you set (daily, weekly, monthly) and downloads only the new videos.
- **Archive** - everything you downloaded, in three vaults: Videos, Audio and Playlists.
- **Library** - your favourite videos sorted in categories such as Cinema or Music, each with its own icon.
- **Details** - the description of every video is saved with it, together with direct links to its folder and its web page.
- **Space check** - before a download starts the application compares the estimated size with the free space of the target drive and warns you in time.
- **Live gauges** - network speed, application speed, downloaded size, free disk space, disk activity and the download queue.
- **Two languages** - English and Arabic, switched from the globe icon.
